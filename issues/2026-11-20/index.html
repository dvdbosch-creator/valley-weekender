Figueroa Mountain Turns 16↗

Fig Mountain celebrates 16 years at the original Buellton taproom, the brewery's mothership on Industrial Way, with a free, Grease-themed anniversary party. Expect live music, a bounce house, carnival games, a classic car show, and a 16th anniversary limited bottle release.

FreeFamilyfigmtnbrew.com

CalNAM Holiday Marketplace↗

California Nature Art Museum, Mission Dr

Nearly 20 vendors with mounted insects, glass art, wildlife prints, vintage goods and more, on a free admission day. Sangeetha Gopalakrishnan, whose new exhibition is on the third floor, meets visitors from 10 AM to 1 PM.

FreeFamily

Site Specific Mantlepiece Workshop↗

Site Specific, 466 Bell St, Los Alamos

The Los Alamos pottery and architecture studio is on winter hiatus, off making work for 2027 and reopening in March, but it is hosting one holiday workshop the weekend after Thanksgiving. Carve tiny houses from a block of clay for a handmade mantlepiece display: Fri, Nov 27 or Sat, Nov 28 from 1 to 2:30 PM, or Sun, Nov 29 from 5 to 6 PM. Tools, materials and firing are included, all levels welcome, ages 16 and up.

$100potteryandhouses.com
