A sunny, crisp Friday, clouds on Saturday and rain likely on Sunday. Figueroa Mountain throws a Grease-themed 16th birthday party in Buellton, holiday shopping kicks off in Solvang, and Rachael & Vilray play the Lobero. Thanksgiving is next week, so the turkeys and the Wednesday plans are in here too.

Read the full issue: The Valley Weekender, Nov 20 to 22, 2026

Valley weather: Friday 57 and sunny, Saturday 64 with increasing clouds, Sunday 59 with rain likely.

Top Picks

Figueroa Mountain Turns 16. Saturday 2 to 9 PM, Buellton, free. Fig Mountain celebrates 16 years at its original Buellton taproom with a Grease-themed party: live music, a bounce house, carnival games, a classic car show and a 16th anniversary limited bottle release. Bring the whole family.

CalNAM Holiday Marketplace. Saturday 10 AM to 4 PM, California Nature Art Museum, Solvang, free admission day. Nearly 20 vendors bring mounted insects, glass art, wildlife prints, vintage goods and more, and Gopalakrishnan, whose new exhibition is on the third floor, meets visitors from 10 AM to 1 PM.

Rachael & Vilray. Friday 7:30 PM, Lobero Theatre, Santa Barbara, $55 to $100. Price of Lake Street Dive and guitarist and songwriter Vilray blend traditional pop, jazz and theatrical storytelling, with songs from their album West of Broadway.

Evening and Music

Rebel Heart and Dani and the Delinquents at the Maverick. Maverick Saloon, Santa Ynez. Friday starts with line dance lessons from 6 to 8 PM ($10), then Rebel Heart play country from 8:30 to 11:30 PM. Saturday, Dani and the Delinquents bring rockabilly, jump blues, R&B and swing from 8:30, and Sunday is the Sunday Roundup with all-day specials and happy hour from 3 to 6 PM.

Mary Poppins. SYHS Little Theater, Santa Ynez. The high school theatre group closes its run Friday at 6:30 PM, Saturday at 1 and 6:30 PM, and with a Sunday matinee at 1 PM. A sweet night out for the whole family.

Grupo Bryndis & Guardianes del Amor. Friday 8 PM, Chumash Casino Resort, Santa Ynez, $40 to $60, 21+. The two share the bill in the Samala Showroom, and the casino suggests arriving an hour early.

Los Olivos Winery Events

Out of the Blue at the Carhartt Cabin. Friday 5 to 8 PM in downtown Los Olivos. Jackson Gillies plays Sunday afternoon with no reservations and no cover (times at the link), and next Wednesday, Nov 25, the Cabin celebrates Thanksgiving Eve with Jayden Secor from 5 to 8 PM.

Fess Parker Turkey Trot. Saturday 10 AM, Fess Parker Winery and Home Ranch. A family-friendly 5K run or walk across the ranch's upper and lower mesas, benefiting the Foodbank of Santa Barbara County.

Refugio Ranch Concert Series. Live music every weekend through Nov 29 at the Grand Ave tasting room, with eats from the food truck. The full lineup is at the link.

A Month of Mattei's. The Inn at Mattei's Tavern keeps its 140th birthday month going. Friday has The Art of Uni from 3 to 4 PM and Pint-Sized Pioneers from 3 to 6 PM, and Saturday has Sip Fine Wine from 3 to 4 PM, then Felix After Hours from 8 to 11 PM.

Wednesday Winemaker Takeover: Andrew Murray. Wednesday, Nov 25, 5 to 7 PM, Tavern Bar at Mattei's. The night before Thanksgiving, Murray and family pour select vintages and a signature cocktail he chose himself, to live local music. $10 a glass or cocktail, 21+, no reservation needed.

Valley Winery Events

Nicholas Escobar at Peake Ranch. Saturday 4:30 to 6:30 PM, Peake Ranch Winery, Buellton, free. The Los Angeles folk singer and songwriter plays songs from his debut album, Echo Park, due in 2027. Bring friends, family and the dog.

Brick Barn Weekend. Brick Barn Wine Estate, Buellton. Out of the Blue play Saturday from 1 to 4 PM, Sunday is Sangria Sunday at $10 a glass from 11 AM to 7 PM with Do No Harm Band from 2 to 4, and Wine Down Wednesday on Nov 25 brings Becker from 4:30 to 6:30 PM with 50% off wines by the glass.

Shop Local

Holiday shopping season starts this weekend, and Thanksgiving is next Thursday. Here is what Valley shops and makers have posted.

Los Olivos Outfitters. 2353 Hollister Street, Suites E and D, Los Olivos. Starting your holiday list? Begin at Josh Hartley's menswear shop, a super special stop for clothing, sunglasses and art under one roof, and a must visit any time you are in Los Olivos.

Site Specific Mantlepiece Workshop. 466 Bell St, Los Alamos, $100. The pottery and architecture studio is on winter hiatus until March, but it is hosting one holiday workshop: carve tiny houses from a block of clay for a handmade mantlepiece display on Fri, Nov 27 or Sat, Nov 28 from 1 to 2:30 PM, or Sun, Nov 29 from 5 to 6 PM. Tools, materials and firing are included, all levels welcome, ages 16 and up.

The Feral Holiday Collection. Made in Santa Ynez. Small-batch gifts made by hand, each with a handwritten note, from a $28 bag of Maple Mallows for teachers to a $244 box of everything Feral makes. Order by Dec 17 for West Coast shipping; local Valley delivery runs through Dec 21.

Motley Crew Marketplace Holiday Turkeys. Buellton. Reserve a pasture-raised turkey from a small farm in the Santa Rita region at $12 a pound ($10 for Motley Crew Club members), with dry brining and spatchcocking available at pickup.

Valley Hardware Open House. Friday 5:30 to 7 PM, 1665 Mission Dr, Solvang. The Solvang hardware store holds its annual open house on Friday evening.

The Island of Misfit Art. Cypress Gallery, Lompoc, through Nov 29. A holiday show of artwork and one-of-a-kind handcrafted gifts by Lompoc's local artists; gallery times at the link.

Markets and holiday fairs: CalNAM Holiday Marketplace (Sat 10 AM to 4 PM), the Book Loft Holiday Open House (Sat 10 AM to 7 PM) and the Grateful Goods Arts and Crafts Sale at Bethania Lutheran (Sat 10 AM to 3 PM, free), all in Solvang, plus the Solvang Farmers Market on Wednesday, Nov 25, 2:30 to 6 PM.

Worth the Drive

Juilliard String Quartet & Simone Dinnerstein. Saturday 7:30 PM, Lobero Theatre, Santa Barbara. The quartet plays Ravel's String Quartet in F Major and Bartok's Fourth, then pianist Dinnerstein joins for Schumann's Piano Quintet. On Sunday at 4 PM the Lobero hosts the Santa Barbara Youth Symphony.

The Wizard of Oz. PCPA, Marian Theatre, Santa Maria. Friday 7 PM, Saturday and Sunday 1:30 and 7 PM. PCPA's holiday show, made with 2 Ring Circus, brings the classic to new heights with aerial stunts and acrobatics, and runs through Dec 20.

Plan Ahead

Thanksgiving Weekend Music at the Wineries. Lincourt has Erinn Alissa on Fri, Nov 27, 2 to 5 PM; Rideau has Heavy Cats on Sat, Nov 28 and Kenny Taylor on Sun, Nov 29, both 1 to 4 PM; and Firestone Vineyard has live music Friday and Saturday (times at the link).

Fesstivity Holiday Soiree. Friday, Dec 4, 4:30 to 6:30 PM, The Bubble Shack, Los Olivos. Light bites, live music from Coderre, and a glass of the newly released Fesstivity Anniversary Cuvee with each ticket.

Olde Fashioned Christmas and the Town Tree Lighting. Saturday, Dec 5, 2 to 7 PM, downtown Los Olivos. Twinkling lights, hot cocoa, festive music, ornament decorating, photos with Santa and shopping local, with the town tree on the flagpole lit at 6 PM.

St. Mark's Preschool Gingerbread Wonderland. Saturday, Dec 5, 4 to 7:30 PM, St. Mark's in the Valley, Los Olivos. A preschool fundraiser with a chili and bake sale; it closes at 6 PM for the tree lighting at the flag pole, then reopens until 7:30 PM.

Mattei's Annual Tree Lighting and Marketplace. Sunday, Dec 6, 4 to 6 PM, $10. A separate, ticketed lighting the next day in Mattei's courtyard, with hot cocoa and cider, ornament decorating, live music, courtyard snow, stagecoach rides and a holiday marketplace. All ticket sales go to People Helping People.

Ornament Making Workshop. Community Clayworks, Buellton, Dec 5, 6 and 12, $55. Make four personalized keepsake ornaments, glazed and fired; ages 5 and up, and every participant must register.

SYV Holiday Lights Festival. Santa Ynez Valley Botanic Garden, Buellton, weekends Dec 4 to 6, 11 to 13 and 18 to 20, 5 to 9 PM. Millions of lights, mini train rides, selfies with Santa and falling snow, and the festival says it will sell out.

New Santa Barbara Bowl Shows. Durand Jones & The Indications and The Teskey Brothers on Friday, July 16, 2027 at 6 PM, and Jason Isbell and the 400 Unit with Drive-By Truckers on Saturday, Aug 28, 2027 at 6:30 PM. Stewart Copeland's Police Deranged with the Santa Barbara Symphony is Friday, June 4, 2027.
