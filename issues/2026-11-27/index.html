Friday, Nov 27 to Sunday, Nov 29, 2026

Thanksgiving weekend opens the holiday season in the Valley. Solvang switches on its Julefest lights Friday, local makers fill the Grange for Black Friday, and the tasting rooms have music all weekend. Expect 81 and sunny on Friday, cooling to 67 by Sunday with a slight chance of rain.

Julefest Lights Up Solvang↗

Solvang's Danish Christmas starts Friday. A light and music show plays in Solvang Park every hour from 5 to 9 PM, Santa's Village opens Saturday and Sunday from noon to 4, and carolers stroll downtown Saturday from 5 to 8.

Santa Ynez Valley Grange, Los Olivos
