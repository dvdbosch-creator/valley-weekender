Firestone Walker Turns 30↗

A whole weekend for the brewery's 30th. Friday is 30 Beers for 30 Years: build your own flight of five from more than 30 brews ($25 with a taster glass). Saturday is a five-course, beer-paired dinner under the Cathedral of Barrels, with stories from founders David Walker, Adam Firestone and Nick Firestone and Brewmaster Matt Brynildson. Sunday wraps up with brunch specials at the Buellton Taproom.

Flight $25Dinner $100firestonewalker.com

Bob's Well Bread, Ballard

Bread-themed still lifes by Mary Kay West and friends from the Central Coast Art League. Meet the artists over Bob's treats and Sunstone wines.

Santa Barbara Symphony at the Granada

Nir Kabaretti leads Berlioz, plus Poulenc's Gloria with soprano Devon Guthrie and the Symphony Chorus, and Debussy's Prelude to the Afternoon of a Faun.

HOKA Pacific Trails California by UTMB↗

Basecamp at 482 Alisal Rd, Solvang, and Rancho San Fernando Rey

Trail runners fill Solvang for the weekend. Basecamp is open downtown Friday 2 to 7 PM and Saturday 10 AM to 5 PM. The 50K starts Saturday at 7 AM and the 25K Sunday at 8 AM on the private ranch. There is no parking at the ranch, so spectators ride the shuttle from Alisal Mesa Road.
