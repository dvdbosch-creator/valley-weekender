Buellton Fall Festival↗

Three days of carnival rides, food and merchant vendors, and more than 40 bands on two stages. Friday is an 80s and 90s dance party with The 90s Babiez at 7 and The Molly Ringwald Project at 8:30, Saturday ends with Reggae Night and Arise Roots at 8:30, and Sunday brings Fiesta Sunday and Country Night, with Jayden Secor closing the main stage at 8:30.

Free admissionFamilybuelltonfallfest.com
